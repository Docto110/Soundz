# Soundz

A tiny soundboard for Android.

- Tap a square to play its sound.
- Tap **+** to record a sound or pick an audio file.
- Hold a square for 5 seconds to delete it.

Every push to `main` builds a new APK automatically. Get the newest one from **Releases** and install it over the old one; your sounds stay.

## Starter sounds

New installs start with a **Welcome to Soundz** button. Its voice clip is made from `voice/lines.txt` by the *Make voice clips* workflow (Piper, Ryan voice) and saved in `www/starter/`.
